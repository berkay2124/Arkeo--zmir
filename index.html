Journey Through Time

3000+ years of continuous human civilization

3000-1200 BCE

Bronze Age

Early settlements emerge along the Aegean coast, establishing trade networks.

Sites: Liman Tepe, Bakla Tepe
1200-480 BCE

Archaic Period

Foundation of major Ionian cities and emergence of Greek culture.

Sites: Smyrna, Klazomenai, Teos
480-323 BCE

Classical Period

Height of Greek city-states, philosophy, and architectural innovation.

Sites: Erythrai, Notion, Lebedos
323-30 BCE

Hellenistic Period

Expansion under Alexander and his successors, cultural synthesis.

Sites: Pergamon, Metropolis, Magnesia
30 BCE-330 CE

Roman Period

Integration into Roman Empire, monumental architecture and prosperity.

Sites: Ephesus, Smyrna Agora, Allianoi
330-1453 CE

Byzantine Period

Christian transformation, basilicas, and continued urban life.

Sites: Basilica of St. John, Sirince
