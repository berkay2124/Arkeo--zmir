About This Project

Web-Based Digitization of Archaeological Sites in Izmir Province

Project Overview

This digital platform addresses a significant gap in cultural heritage management by creating an accessible, structured resource that serves both academic researchers and cultural tourism audiences. Through systematic data collection from official sources and rigorous verification processes, the project has established a comprehensive database of 40 archaeological sites across Izmir Province.

Academic Research

95% of content verified with academic citations. Comprehensive excavation histories and bibliographies for serious research.

International Access

English language platform maximizing global accessibility to Turkey's archaeological heritage.

Dual-Audience Design

Layered information architecture serving both casual tourists and academic researchers.

Lesser-Known Sites

22 lesser-known sites documented with equal rigor to famous destinations like Ephesus.

Research Questions

RQ1: Information Structuring

How can scattered archaeological information be systematically structured and presented through a web-based platform to serve both academic and general audiences effectively?

RQ2: Design Principles

What design principles and technical approaches best support a dual-content strategy that maintains academic rigor while ensuring accessibility for cultural tourists?

RQ3: Digital Visibility

How can digital platforms contribute to increasing visibility and appreciation for lesser-known archaeological sites?

Methodology

This project employs Design Science Research (DSR) methodology with Agile development principles, organized into 14-week sprint cycles.

Data Collection

40 sites documented from official Ministry publications and academic sources

Verification

Two-source verification protocol with 127 academic references

Development

Modern web technologies with responsive, accessible design

Testing

User acceptance testing with academic and tourist users
